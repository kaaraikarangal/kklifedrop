# National Blood Transfusion Council (NBTC)
# Guidelines for Blood Donor Selection and Blood Donor Referral (February 2025)
**Director General of Health Services | Ministry of Health and Family Welfare | Government of India, New Delhi**

---

## 1. Introduction & Core Principles
* **Primary Responsibility of Blood Transfusion Service (BTS)**: Provide a safe and sufficient supply of blood and blood components to those in need, ensuring the act causes no harm to the donor and minimal risk to the recipient.
* **Scope**: Applies to whole blood donors and apheresis donors (red cells, platelets, plasma, and other components).
* **Core Principles of Donor Engagement**:
  1. Blood should be accepted **only from voluntary, non-remunerated, low-risk, safe, and healthy donors**. Replacement donors are being phased out.
  2. Maintain and retain healthy repeat donors through constant engagement, recognition, and appreciation.
  3. Pre-donation donor screening with standardized questionnaire and health check-up by a qualified Medical Officer (minimum MBBS).
  4. Ensure confidential unit exclusion and self-deferral options at any stage before, during, or after donation.

---

## 2. The 5 Stages of Blood Donor Counselling

### Stage 1: Pre-Donation Information
Educate prospective donors about:
* The nature and clinical use of blood/components and importance of healthy lifestyle.
* Eligibility criteria, rationale for questionnaire, and health check-up.
* Option to withdraw or self-defer at any time before, during, or post-donation.
* The blood donation process and potential adverse reactions.
* Transfusion Transmissible Infections (TTI), modes of transmission, and the window period.
* Mandatory screening tests performed on donated blood and possible consequences of abnormal results.

### Stage 2: Pre-Donation Counselling
* One-on-one private counselling session.
* Clarify questions on the Donor Questionnaire and ensure truthful disclosure.
* Reiterate understanding of TTI testing and disclosure of results.
* Explain self-deferral, temporary deferral, and permanent deferral.
* Obtain donor's informed written consent.

### Stage 3: Donor Questionnaire & Medical Assessment
* Administered in English or local language (assistance provided for illiterate donors).
* Donor's demographic details, date, and time recorded.
* Basic health checkup by a Medical Officer (MBBS): history taking, vital signs, Hb estimation, and targeted physical examination.
* Final medical fitness decision rests strictly with the Medical Officer.

### 5-Point Donor Informed Consent Declaration:
1. Blood donation is a voluntary act with no inducement or remuneration.
2. It is a medical procedure and the donor voluntarily accepts associated procedural risks.
3. Donated blood and recovered plasma may be sent for plasma fractionation for manufacturing life-saving plasma-derived medicines.
4. Blood will be tested for HIV 1 & 2, Hepatitis B, Hepatitis C, Syphilis, Malaria parasites, and other mandated safety tests.
5. The donor consents to be contacted and informed in case of any abnormal test results.

### Stage 4: Counselling During Blood Donation
* Ensure donor comfort during venepuncture and collection.
* Reduce donor anxiety and minimize risk of vasovagal adverse reactions (fainting).
* Foster trust and confidence; thank donor for their humanitarian gift.

### Stage 5: Post-Donation Care & Advice
* Rest for 10–15 minutes in the observation area; consume refreshments and plenty of fluids.
* Avoid strenuous physical work, heavy lifting, or sports for the rest of the day.
* No smoking for at least 30 minutes; no driving or operating machinery immediately post-donation.
* Keep the venepuncture bandage on for at least 6 hours.
* If feeling dizzy or faint, sit down and place head between knees or lie flat with feet elevated.
* Contact details of the Blood Centre provided in case of post-donation discomfort.
* Blood donor card/certificate issued; donor encouraged to return as a regular repeat donor.

## 3. Complete Blood Donor Selection Criteria (S.No. 1 to 101)

### Category A: General Physical & Vital Signs (S.No. 1 to 12)
1. **Well-being**: Good health, mentally alert, physically fit. Differently-abled donors with communication/sight difficulties accepted if clear communication and valid informed consent are established.
2. **Age**:
   - Minimum: **18 years**.
   - Maximum: **65 years**.
   - First-time donors: Upper limit **60 years**.
   - Repeat donors: Upper limit **65 years**.
   - Apheresis donors: **18 to 60 years**.
3. **Weight & Collection Volume**:
   - 350 ml whole blood: Minimum **45 kg**.
   - 450 ml whole blood: Minimum **> 55 kg**.
   - Single platelet apheresis: Minimum **50 kg**.
   - Double platelet apheresis: Minimum **60 kg**.
   - Double red cell apheresis: Minimum **60 kg**.
4. **Donation Intervals**:
   - Whole blood: **Every 3 months (90 days)** for males; **Every 4 months (120 days)** for females.
   - Single apheresis (platelet/plasma): At least **48 hours** interval (max 2 times/week, max 4 times/month, max 24 times/year).
   - Double plateletpheresis (≥6×10¹¹ platelets): At least **7 days** interval (max 2 times/month, 12 times/year; platelet count ≥ 250×10³/µL).
   - Whole blood to plateletpheresis: Minimum **28 days**.
   - Plateletpheresis to whole blood: Minimum **7 days** (if complete red cell reinfusion; otherwise 90 days).
5. **Blood Pressure**:
   - Systolic: **100 – 140 mm Hg**.
   - Diastolic: **60 – 90 mm Hg**.
   - Acceptable with or without antihypertensive medications provided dose/drug unchanged in last 28 days, no end-organ damage, and no fainting/giddiness.
6. **Pulse**: **60 – 100 beats/min**, regular rhythm.
7. **Temperature**: Afebrile; normal **37°C (98.4°F)**.
8. **Respiration**: Free from acute respiratory disease.
9. **Hemoglobin (Hb)**:
   - Whole blood: **≥ 12.5 g/dL**.
   - Double red cell apheresis: **≥ 14.0 g/dL**.
   - Thalassemia minor/trait: Acceptable if Hb ≥ 12.5 g/dL.
10. **Meals & Fasting**: Donor must **NOT be fasting** or observing a fast. Last light meal must have been taken within **4 hours** before donation.
11. **Alcohol Consumption**: Donor must not have regular heavy alcohol intake and must **NOT show signs of intoxication**. Must wait at least 24 hours after alcohol intake and be fully sober.
12. **Occupation Safety**: Aircrew, long-distance drivers, divers, miners, emergency workers: No donation for **12 hours** after duty, and must not resume strenuous work for at least **24 hours** post-donation. Night shift workers must have adequate restful sleep.

### Category B: High-Risk Behavior & Exposure (S.No. 13 to 15)
13. **Risk Behaviour**: Free from diseases transmissible by blood. Permanently deferred: Transgender, Men who have sex with men (MSM), Female sex workers (FSW), Injecting drug users (IDU), persons with multiple sex partners, or prison/jail inmates.
14. **Travel & Residence**: Defer if travel/residence in areas endemic for TTI without Indian screening guidelines. Foreign residents accepted if all criteria met (interpreter required if language barrier exists).
15. **Donor Skin**: Phlebotomy site free of skin lesions or rashes. Forearms free of scars/needle punctures indicative of IV drug abuse or commercial donation.

### Category C: Women's Health & Physiological Status (S.No. 16 to 19)
16. **Pregnancy / Delivery**: Defer for **12 months** after delivery.
17. **Abortion / Miscarriage**: Defer for **6 months** after abortion.
18. **Breastfeeding / Lactation**: Defer for **12 months** after delivery.
19. **Menstruation**: Defer for the **period of menstruation** (acceptable once cycle ends and asymptomatic).

### Category D: Respiratory & Non-Specific Illnesses (S.No. 20 to 23)
20. **Non-specific Symptoms (Fever, Malaise, Pain)**: Defer until all symptoms subside and donor is afebrile. If fever with joint pain, rashes, or petechiae: Defer for **28 days** after recovery.
21. **Cold, Flu, Cough, Sore Throat, Acute Sinusitis**: Defer until all symptoms subside and donor is afebrile.
22. **Chronic Sinusitis**: Acceptable unless currently taking antibiotics.
23. **Asthma**:
    - Asymptomatic on maintenance non-steroid or inhaled steroids: **ACCEPTABLE**.
    - Acute exacerbation or on oral/injectable steroids: Defer for **14 days** after recovery.
    - Severe chronic obstructive/restrictive lung disease: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.

### Category E: Surgical Procedures (S.No. 24 to 29)
24. **Major Surgery** (GA/spinal, hospitalization, significant blood loss): Defer for **12 months** after recovery.
25. **Minor Surgery**: Defer for **3 months** after recovery.
26. **Received Blood Transfusion**: Defer for **12 months** from transfusion date.
27. **Open Heart / Bypass Surgery**: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
28. **Cancer Surgery**: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
29. **Dental Procedures**:
    - Simple scaling, filling, cleaning: Defer **48 hours**.
    - Endodontic procedure (Root Canal Treatment / Tooth extraction): Defer **14 days**.
    - Bone graft / surgical dental reconstruction: Defer **12 months**.

### Category F: Cardiovascular Diseases (S.No. 30 to 36)
30. **Active Cardiac Symptoms** (Chest pain, shortness of breath, swollen feet): **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
31. **Myocardial Infarction (Heart Attack)**: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
32. **Cardiac Medications** (Digitalis, Nitroglycerin): **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
33. **Hypertensive Heart Disease**: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
34. **Coronary Artery Disease**: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
35. **Angina Pectoris**: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
36. **Rheumatic Heart Disease with residual damage**: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.

### Category G: Central Nervous System & Psychiatric (S.No. 37 to 40)
37. **Migraine**: Acceptable if mild and occurs less than once a week.
38. **Convulsions & Epilepsy**: Acceptable if off medication and seizure-free for at least **3 years**. Permanently deferred if organic brain cause.
39. **Schizophrenia**: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
40. **Anxiety & Mood Disorders (Depression, Bipolar)**: Acceptable if stable and feeling well on the day, regardless of medication.

### Category H: Endocrine Disorders (S.No. 41 to 44)
41. **Diabetes Mellitus**:
    - Controlled by diet or oral medications (no dosage change in last 4 weeks, no neuropathy or ulcers): **ACCEPTABLE**.
    - On Insulin injection: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
    - Complications with multi-organ involvement: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
42. **Thyroid Disorders**:
    - Benign thyroid disorder (e.g. Hypothyroidism on Levothyroxine): **ACCEPTABLE** if euthyroid, asymptomatic, and dose stable for 8 weeks.
    - Thyrotoxicosis (Hyperthyroidism): **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
    - Malignant thyroid tumors: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
43. **Polycystic Ovarian Syndrome (PCOS)**: **ACCEPTABLE** if well on donation day and treatment meets medication criteria.
44. **Other Endocrine Disorders**: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.

### Category I: Liver Diseases & Hepatitis (S.No. 45 to 50)
45. **Hepatitis**:
    - Known Hepatitis B or Hepatitis C (or unknown hepatitis): **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
    - Known Hepatitis A or E: Defer for **12 months** after recovery.
46. **Spouse / Close Contact of Hepatitis B or C**: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
47. **Tattoos, Body Piercing, Acupuncture, Scarification, Invasive Cosmetic Procedures**: Defer for **12 months** (by self or spouse/partner).
48. **Spouse / Partner of Blood Transfusion Recipient**: Defer for **12 months**.
49. **Jaundice**: Acceptable **12 months (1 year)** after acute jaundice resolved with treatment.
50. **Chronic Liver Disease / Cirrhosis / Liver Failure**: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.

### Category J: HIV & Sexually Transmitted Infections (S.No. 51 to 55)
51. **High Risk for HIV** (Transgender, MSM, FSW, IDU, multiple partners): **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
52. **Known HIV-positive person or spouse/partner of PLHA**: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
53. **Symptoms Suggestive of AIDS** (Lymphadenopathy, prolonged fever/diarrhea): **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
54. **Syphilis** (Genital sore, generalized rashes): **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
55. **Gonorrhea**: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.

### Category K: Other Infectious Diseases (S.No. 56 to 67)
56. **Measles, Mumps, Chickenpox**: Defer for **2 weeks (14 days)** after full recovery.
57. **Malaria**: Defer for **3 months** after full recovery and treatment completion.
58. **Typhoid**: Defer for **12 months** after full recovery.
59. **Dengue / Chikungunya / Zika**: Defer for **6 months** after recovery. Travel to Zika outbreak zone: Defer for **28 days**.
60. **Tuberculosis (TB)**: Defer for **2 years (24 months)** following confirmation of cure.
61. **Leishmaniasis (Kala-azar)**: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
62. **Leprosy**: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
63. **Conjunctivitis**: Defer for the period of illness and topical medication.
64. **Osteomyelitis**: Defer for **2 years** after cure and completion of treatment.
65. **Acute Kidney Infection (Pyelonephritis)**: Defer for **6 months** after complete recovery and last medicine.
66. **Acute Cystitis / Urinary Tract Infection (UTI)**: Defer for **2 weeks** after complete recovery and last medicine.
67. **Chronic Kidney Disease / Renal Failure**: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.

### Category L: Digestive System (S.No. 68 to 70)
68. **Diarrhea**: If history in preceding week (especially with fever), defer for **2 weeks** after full recovery and last medication.
69. **GI Endoscopy**:
    - No sampling/biopsy: Defer **7 days**.
    - Benign biopsy: Defer **28 days**.
    - Malignant biopsy: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
    - H. Pylori / other: Defer until treatment completed.
70. **Acid Peptic Disease**:
    - Mild acid reflux, GERD, mild hiatus hernia: **ACCEPTABLE**.
    - Stomach ulcer with symptoms or recurrent bleeding: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.

### Category M: Systemic & Blood Disorders (S.No. 71 to 76)
71. **Autoimmune Disorders** (SLE, Scleroderma, Dermatomyositis, Ankylosing Spondylitis, Severe Rheumatoid Arthritis): **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
72. **Polycythemia**:
    - Primary Polycythemia Vera: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
    - Secondary Polycythemia (high altitude): Acceptable if Hb < 18.5 g/dL in males and < 16.5 g/dL in females.
73. **Bleeding Disorders & Unexplained Bleeding Tendency**: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
74. **Malignancy / Cancer**: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
75. **Allergic Disorders**:
    - Allergy to blood collection substance or cold urticaria: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
    - History of severe allergy: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
    - On oral/injectable steroids: Defer for **28 days** after full recovery and cessation of steroids.
76. **Hemoglobinopathies & Red Cell Enzyme Deficiencies** (with anemia): **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.

### Category N: Vaccinations & Inoculations (S.No. 77 to 80)
77. **Non-live Vaccines & Toxoids** (Typhoid injectable, Cholera, HPV, Influenza, Meningococcal, Pertussis, Pneumococcal, Polio injectable, Diphtheria, Tetanus, COVID non-live, Swine Flu): Defer for **14 days**.
78. **Live Attenuated Vaccines** (Polio oral, MMR, Yellow Fever, Japanese Encephalitis, Influenza oral, Typhoid oral, Cholera oral, Hepatitis A, COVID live attenuated): Defer for **28 days**.
79. **Antisera** (Anti-tetanus serum, Anti-snake venom serum, Anti-diphtheria serum, Anti-gas gangrene serum): Defer for **28 days**.
80. **Rabies Vaccine (Post Animal Bite) & Immunoglobulins** (Anti-rabies vaccine post-bite, Hepatitis B Immunoglobulin): Defer for **1 year (12 months)**.

### Category O: Medications & Drugs (S.No. 81 to 99)
81. **Oral Contraceptives**: **ACCEPTABLE**.
82. **Analgesics (Paracetamol)**: **ACCEPTABLE**.
83. **Vitamins & Nutritional Supplements**: **ACCEPTABLE**.
84. **Mild Sedatives & Tranquilizers**: **ACCEPTABLE**.
85. **Allopurinol (Gout)**: **ACCEPTABLE**.
86. **Cholesterol-lowering Medications (Statins)**: **ACCEPTABLE** (except monoclonal antibody therapy).
87. **Salicylates (Aspirin) & other NSAIDs**: Defer for **3 days** if blood is used for platelet component preparation. Acceptable for whole blood.
88. **Antifungals (Ketoconazole) & Antihelmintics (Mebendazole)**: Defer for **7 days** after last dose.
89. **Antibiotics**: Defer for **2 weeks (14 days)** after last dose if well.
90. **Antiplatelets (Ticlopidine, Clopidogrel)**: Defer for **2 weeks (14 days)** after last dose.
91. **Piroxicam, Dipyridamole**: Defer for **2 weeks (14 days)** after last dose.
92. **Acne Meds (Isotretinoin, Retinoids)**: Defer for **1 month** after last dose. **Etretinate**: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
93. **Finasteride (BPH / Hair Loss)**: Defer for **1 month** after last dose.
94. **Radioactive Materials / Contrast**:
    - Non-malignant condition: Defer for **6 months**.
    - Malignant condition: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
95. **Dutasteride (BPH / Hair Loss)**: Defer for **6 months** after last dose.
96. **Unknown Medication**: Defer until full details/prescription are reviewed.
97. **Oral Anti-diabetic Drugs**: **ACCEPTABLE** if no alteration in dose within last 4 weeks.
98. **Insulin**: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
99. **High-risk Medications** (Anti-arrhythmic, Anticonvulsants, Anticoagulants, Anti-thyroid drugs, Cytotoxic/Chemotherapy, Cardiac failure drugs like Digitalis): **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.

### Category P: Transplants, Cosmetic Procedures & Fainting (S.No. 100 to 101)
100. **Transplants & Tissue Grafts**:
    - Allogeneic / Autologous Organ or Stem Cell transplant: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.
    - Hair Transplant, PRP Therapy, Skin Grafting: Defer for **6 months** after recovery with no ongoing follow-up or medications.
101. **Fainting & Vasovagal Reactions**:
    - Unexplained delayed faint, delayed faint with physical injury, or two consecutive faints following blood donation: **PERMANENTLY DEFERRED**.

## 4. Recall and Referral Mechanism for Sero-reactive Donors

### Information on Test Results
1. Blood donors who consented to be contacted must be recalled if test results are initial sero-reactive for any TTI.
2. Provide private, non-judgmental post-donation counselling prior to referral.
3. Adequate, documented efforts must be made to contact reactive donors.
4. Non-reactive result-seeking donors should also be reassured and counselled to maintain safe behaviors.
5. Licensed blood centers must maintain an updated directory of linked **Integrated Counselling and Testing Centres (ICTC)** and STI clinics.

### Blood Centre Duties & Legal Safeguards
* **Consent**: Obtained in writing prior to donation.
* **Testing of Triage**: Screening in blood centers is a test of triage (Strategy I, A0) to ensure recipient safety. It is **NOT** a clinical diagnosis.
* **Confidentiality**: **Results are NEVER disclosed over the telephone**.
* **Referral Form**: Standard written referral slip and donor consent for referral to avoid litigation due to discordant screening vs confirmatory results.
* **Non-responsive Donors**: If donor fails to return despite **3 consecutive weekly attempts**, the HIV sero-reactive donor's details are shared with the linked ICTC under shared confidentiality.

### HIV Testing Strategy at Blood Centres (Strategy I)
* Whole blood units undergo one test of high sensitivity (A0).
* If non-reactive (A0-): Unit accepted as free of HIV.
* If reactive (A0+): Unit is considered HIV positive, discarded immediately, and the donor is recalled for counselling and confirmatory referral.

### Other TTI Referral Protocols (HBV, HCV, Syphilis, Malaria)
* Sero-reactive units discarded immediately.
* Referred to associated hospital Outpatient Departments (OPD), Gastroenterology, or STI clinics for clinical evaluation and confirmatory testing.
